AI-Powered Ideation, Development & Experimentation by Evolv AI

How Evolv AI uses artificial intelligence to power every stage of digital experience optimization — and why Addison Applications is the right partner to implement it.

What is Evolv AI?

Evolv AI is a SaaS solution that allows companies of all sizes to use the power of AI to come up with, develop, and test improvements in their user experience. Feed Evolv AI's platform your brand guidelines, product documentation, marketing research, experimentation results, and analytics — and it will use those insights alongside computer vision and generative AI to evaluate the effectiveness of your digital experiences against specific audience segments or goals.

It then suggests a roadmap of digital experience improvements that gets continuously updated based on new data as it becomes available. Think of it as an always-on optimization strategist powered by machine learning.

What type of companies can use Evolv AI?

Any company with goals for their website can use Evolv AI. Whether you're focused on B2B lead generation, eCommerce conversion, or subscription-based services, you can define the goals you want to improve — and Evolv will use AI to generate a prioritized list of suggested changes.

No minimum traffic requirement. You don't need enterprise-level volume to start benefiting from AI-powered suggestions. Evolv AI's pricing model is designed with plans that work for customers of all sizes.

Does Evolv AI work with Shopify?

Yes — Evolv AI works with Shopify websites, and Addison Applications has seen real business impact for Shopify customers verified through A/B and multivariate testing. We're experts at implementing Evolv AI on Shopify sites and then updating the user experience to drive measurable improvements in:

  • Conversion Rate — turning more visitors into buyers
  • Average Order Value — increasing revenue per transaction
  • Customer Engagement — reducing bounce and improving time on site

How Evolv AI & Addison Applications partner together

Addison Applications has an official partnership with Evolv AI — and on top of that, our Director of Optimization worked at Evolv AI for close to 3 years. We know the tool inside and out, and have seen it drive real business value for companies both large and small.

We've delivered results for one of the largest telecom companies in the world as well as a Shopify customer in the nutraceutical industry. Whether you're enterprise or mid-market, we can help you:

  • Implement Evolv AI on your website with proper configuration
  • Develop and refine AI-suggested UX enhancements
  • Code custom changes and launch experiments that move revenue
